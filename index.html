How the stack works

LIFO · Last In, First Out

What is a stack?

A stack is a linear data structure that follows the Last In, First Out principle. Think of a pile of trays: you add and remove items from the top.

SmartPark's stack

Each arriving vehicle is added with push(). The newest vehicle sits at the top and can leave using pop(). peek() inspects the top without removing it.

What if a vehicle is underneath?

A standard stack cannot remove an item from the middle directly. SmartPark temporarily pops vehicles above the target, removes the target, then pushes the displaced vehicles back in their original order.
